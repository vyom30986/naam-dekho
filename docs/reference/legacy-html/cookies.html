3. Cookies Policy

A "cookie" is a small text file that a website places on your browser or device. Cookies allow the website to recognise you on subsequent visits, remember your preferences, and enable certain functionality. Some cookies expire at the end of the session ("session cookies"); others persist for a defined period ("persistent cookies"). For simplicity, we use "cookies" to refer to all tracking technologies, including HTML5 local storage, IndexedDB, web beacons / pixel tags, and device fingerprinting.

Consent and management

  • On your first visit, a cookie-consent banner is presented. You may (a) accept all cookies, (b) reject all non-essential, or (c) customise category-level consent.
  • Your choice is stored for 12 months in a strictly-necessary cookie (nd_cookie_consent), after which you are re-prompted.
  • You may revoke or modify consent at any time by clicking the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.
  • Strictly-necessary cookies cannot be disabled through this mechanism — you may disable them through browser settings, but this will likely render the Platform non-functional.

Browser-level controls

Most modern browsers allow you to view, manage, delete and block cookies:

  • Chrome — support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647
  • Firefox — support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer
  • Safari — support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac
  • Edge — support.microsoft.com

Please note that disabling cookies, particularly strictly-necessary cookies, may impair or prevent the use of the Platform.

Do Not Track signals

Some browsers transmit "Do Not Track" signals. As there is no industry-consensus interpretation, we do not respond to them at present. You retain all controls described above.

Updates to this Cookies Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The Effective Date at the top indicates the last update. We will re-prompt for consent if our use of cookies materially expands.
