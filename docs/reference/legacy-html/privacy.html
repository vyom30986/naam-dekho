1. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Naam Dekho Technologies Private Limited ("Naam Dekho", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares and protects the Personal Data of its users. It is published pursuant to and in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act"), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

⌘ Plain-language summary

Free-tier searches collect almost nothing — only your IP address and the name you typed, both for a short period and only to fight abuse and improve quality. Paid tiers collect your phone number, email and payment information so we can sign you in, charge you, and deliver your PDF report. We do not sell your data. We do not train models on your data. We store as little as possible, for as short as possible.

Data we collect

Automatically collected

  • IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, screen resolution
  • Referring URL, page navigation timestamps, clickstream data within the Platform
  • Approximate geographic location derived from IP (city / state granularity)
  • Cookie identifiers — see our Cookies Policy

Provided by you

  • The proposed business or baby name being checked ("Search Input")
  • Optional industry/category indicator
  • Phone number — required for sign-in to Paid Tiers (Firebase Authentication or MSG91 via OTP)
  • Email address — captured at sign-in or for receipt and PDF delivery
  • Display name, profile image (if voluntarily provided)
  • Payment metadata — gateway transaction ID, amount, time, status. We do NOT store card numbers, CVV, UPI handles, account numbers, or full bank details — those are tokenised by Razorpay (primary) or Paytm (fallback) under RBI directions
  • Agency lead-form data — name, role, company, firm type, email, phone, volume estimate, budget range, notes
  • Support communications — emails, chats, call recordings (where lawful)

We do NOT collect

  • Children's data. Baby mode is for parents/guardians. We do not collect any date of birth, photograph, school, address, biometric or health information of a child
  • Sensitive personal data. No financial-account, biometric, genetic, sexual-orientation, religion, political-belief, criminal-record or trade-union data
  • Free-Tier profiles. Free-Tier Search Inputs are not associated with a user identity beyond the IP address; we do not build profiles on Free-Tier users

Who we share with

We do not sell, rent or trade Personal Data. We share only with the following limited categories of recipients, each contractually bound to use the data solely for the purposes for which we shared it:

  • Payment processors — Razorpay (primary) and Paytm Payment Services (fallback) — both RBI-licensed Payment Aggregators
  • Authentication providers — Firebase Authentication and/or MSG91 — strictly to deliver and verify OTPs
  • Infrastructure providers — AWS India (managed PG/S3), Cloudflare (edge, R2), Hetzner (compute) — all data encrypted at rest
  • Observability vendors — Sentry, Grafana Labs, Better Stack — logs scrubbed of Personal Data where feasible
  • Professional advisors — CAs, lawyers, auditors — bound by professional confidentiality
  • Law-enforcement / regulators — only on lawful order or compulsion
  • Successors in interest — in case of merger/acquisition, the successor is bound by terms no less protective than this policy

Third-Party Sources are NOT data recipients

When the Platform checks a Third-Party Source (e.g. MCA21, IP India), no Personal Data of yours is transmitted to that source. The query consists only of the Search Input — the proposed name string — together with the technical headers necessary for the request. Your identity remains private to the Platform.

Cross-border data transfers

Some of our infrastructure providers operate facilities outside India. To the extent that Personal Data is processed in a foreign jurisdiction, such processing is undertaken in compliance with Section 16 of the DPDP Act. We give preference to providers offering India-region data residency (AWS Mumbai, Cloudflare APAC).

Your rights as a Data Principal

Under Chapter III of the DPDP Act, you have the following rights, which we honour through dedicated channels:

  • Right to information — about categories processed, recipients shared with, and a description of such data (§11)
  • Right to correction / erasure of inaccurate or unnecessary data (§12)
  • Right to nominate another individual to exercise rights on your behalf in case of death or incapacity (§13)
  • Right of grievance redressal (§14)
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time (§6(4))
  • Acknowledgement within 48 hours; substantive response within 30 days. Write to grievance@naamdekho.in

Security of your data

We implement reasonable security practices commensurate with the nature and purposes of processing, in line with IT Rules 2011 Rule 8 and DPDP Act §8(5):

  • TLS 1.3 on all User-facing endpoints; HSTS; secure cookies; content-security policy
  • AES-256 at-rest encryption for PostgreSQL, R2/S3 and full-disk compute
  • Column-level encryption (pgcrypto) for phone and email columns
  • Role-based access control, least-privilege, multi-factor auth for staff, hardware keys for production
  • VPC segmentation, security groups, bastion-only SSH
  • Quarterly third-party pen-test, continuous dependency scanning, secure-code review
  • Incident-response plan with 72-hour Data Protection Board notification under DPDP §8(6)

Children's data

The Platform is not directed at children under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly process the Personal Data of any child. Baby mode is intended for parents and guardians; the only data collected through Baby mode is the proposed name, which is not associated with any identifiable child. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information that could identify a minor, write to grievance@naamdekho.in and we will promptly delete such information.
