Pricing questions

Anything else? Email hello@naamdekho.in.

Why is the free tier really free? Where's the catch?
No catch. The free tier surfaces verdicts using the public-search endpoints of each source. The paid tier opens warmed proxy sessions with CAPTCHA solving on the registers (MCA, IP India, Copyright) that block bots — and produces a PDF you can hand to a CA or advocate. Most users get what they need on free.
Do I need to sign up for the free tier?
No. Just type a name on the home page. Sign-in is required only for paid scans (so we can email you the PDF and your scan history).
What payment methods do you accept?
UPI (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm), all major credit and debit cards via Razorpay, and net banking from 50+ Indian banks. For agency contracts we accept NEFT/RTGS with proper GST invoicing.
Are GST and other taxes included in the prices?
Prices shown are inclusive of GST at applicable rates. A GST-compliant invoice is generated automatically and emailed within minutes of payment.
Can I get a refund if the result wasn't useful?
Yes — we offer a full no-questions-asked refund within 24 hours of the scan, provided the PDF has not been downloaded more than three times. Write to support@naamdekho.in. For agency subscriptions, refunds are prorated.
Is the agency price genuinely as low as ₹3 per name?
For very large annual commitments (50,000+ names), yes. The exact per-name price depends on volume, classes of TM cover requested, white-labelling, API usage, and contract length. Submit the form above and we'll send a written quote.
