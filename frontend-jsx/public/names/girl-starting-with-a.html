Where names starting with A sit in the Avakahada chakra

Questions parents ask

How many girls' names starting with A are on this page?

25, out of 66 published names beginning with A. The gender is the one our corpus records for the name in common Indian usage, not a rule about who may carry it. A further 4 names beginning with A are recorded as unisex and are listed on the letter page rather than here.

Which numerology numbers do these names carry?

They fall across 8 of the nine Chaldean roots — 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8 and 9. The root is the reduction of the whole spelling, so it changes with the spelling: a name written with a doubled vowel and the same name without one can reduce differently, which is worth checking before the spelling goes on a birth certificate.

Does a girl's name have to start with the birth-star syllable?

It is a convention, not a rule. The pandit reads the Moon's nakshatra and pada from the janam kundli and gives the family a syllable; plenty of families treat it as a shortlist and plenty choose the name they love regardless.
