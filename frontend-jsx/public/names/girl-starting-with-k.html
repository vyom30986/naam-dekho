Where names starting with K sit in the Avakahada chakra

The chakra is a table of 108 syllables, not of letters — it is the opening sound that places a name, so one letter can spread across several birth stars.

Opening syllableNamesNakshatraRashi

Khu 3 👂 Shravana · pada 2 Makara
Ki 3 🦌 Mrigashira · pada 4 Mithuna
Ku 2 💧 Ardra · pada 1 Mithuna
Ko 1 🏹 Punarvasu · pada 2 Mithuna

Traditionally the table runs the other way: a pandit reads the birth star and pada from the janam kundli and hands the family a syllable to start the name with. The birth chart is the authority, not the name. This is the reverse lookup, useful once you already have a name in mind.

Questions parents ask

How many girls' names starting with K are on this page?

19, out of 37 published names beginning with K. The gender is the one our corpus records for the name in common Indian usage, not a rule about who may carry it. A further 3 names beginning with K are recorded as unisex and are listed on the letter page rather than here.

Which numerology numbers do these names carry?

They fall across 8 of the nine Chaldean roots — 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8. The root is the reduction of the whole spelling, so it changes with the spelling: a name written with a doubled vowel and the same name without one can reduce differently, which is worth checking before the spelling goes on a birth certificate.

Does a girl's name have to start with the birth-star syllable?

It is a convention, not a rule. The pandit reads the Moon's nakshatra and pada from the janam kundli and gives the family a syllable; plenty of families treat it as a shortlist and plenty choose the name they love regardless.
