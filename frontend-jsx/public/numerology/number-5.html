Chaldean and Pythagorean are not the same system

The two systems in common use disagree on 16 of the 26 letters, which is why a name that reads as 5 here can read as something else on another site. Pythagorean numbers the alphabet in order — A is 1, B is 2, up to I at 9, then J starts again at 1. Chaldean does not follow the alphabet at all: it groups letters by sound, so A, I, J, Q and Y share the value 1, and it never assigns 9 to any letter, that number being held back as sacred. A 9 can only ever arrive in a Chaldean reading through reduction, never from a single letter.

They also stop in different places. Chaldean holds on to the compound total and reads it — 14 as movement & combinations of people, 23 as Royal Star of the Lion and 32 as Magical Power — and only then reduces to the root, so the reading has two layers. Pythagorean reduces straight to one digit, singling out 11, 22 and 33 as master numbers that are left unreduced. And Chaldean is applied to the name as it is actually used and spoken, which is why we compute it on the spelling you type; Pythagorean practice is usually to use the full name as recorded at birth.

Of the 61 names on this page, 6 come out as 5 in the Pythagorean system too and 55 land somewhere else. Neither system is a correction of the other. Naam Dekho computes Chaldean only, and prints every letter value on the numerology index so you can always tell which system a number came from.

Questions people ask

What does name number 5 mean in Chaldean numerology?

5 is the root of any name whose letter values total 5, or total a number that reduces to 5. Chaldean tradition places it under Mercury ☿. The detail sits in the compound total before it is reduced: 14 is read as movement & combinations of people, 23 is read as Royal Star of the Lion and 32 is read as Magical Power. Of the 536 names Naam Dekho publishes, 61 come out as root 5.

Which planet rules number 5?

Mercury, written ☿. Chaldean numerology assigns one of nine planetary rulers to each root, and that assignment is the whole of what the "planet" means here — it is not read from a birth chart and has nothing to do with where Mercury actually was when anyone was born. A rashi comes from the Moon's position; a root number comes from the spelling of the name.

Is name number 5 good for a business?

The tradition marks media, SaaS / Tech, communication, mobility and education favourable for root 5, and banking, insurance or real estate unfavourable. In a live check this is one tile of many, and it is deliberately weak: numerology can return "favourable" or "not traditionally favoured", never a conflict, so it can nudge a score and can never fail a name. A trademark already registered in your class, or a domain someone else owns, is what actually stops a name.

Which birth dates suit name number 5?

Root 5 is traditionally read as harmonious with dates whose digits reduce to 1, 3 or 5 — that is 11 dates of the month: 1, 3, 5, 10, 12, 14, 19, 21, 23, 28, 30. The date is reduced exactly the way the name is: 23 becomes 2 + 3 = 5. Only the day of the month is used, so the month and year do not change the answer.

Why does another calculator give my name a different number?

Almost always because it uses the Pythagorean system rather than the Chaldean one. Pythagorean numbers the alphabet straight through — A is 1, B is 2, on to I at 9, then it starts again — while Chaldean assigns values by sound and never uses 9 at all, treating it as sacred. 16 of the 26 letters are valued differently by the two systems, so the totals rarely match, and a name that is 5 here can be almost anything elsewhere.
