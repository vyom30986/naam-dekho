5. Payment Terms — Razorpay & Paytm

All payments on the Platform are processed by third-party payment gateways. We do not handle, hold or store any payment-instrument data save the transaction metadata returned by the gateway.

  • Primary gateway: Razorpay (Razorpay Software Private Limited) — a Payment Aggregator licensed by the Reserve Bank of India under the Guidelines on Regulation of Payment Aggregators and Payment Gateways dated 17 March 2020.
  • Fallback gateway: Paytm Payment Services Private Limited — also an RBI-licensed Payment Aggregator. The fallback is invoked automatically when the primary is unavailable, returns an unrecoverable error, or rejects your instrument.
  • You will be informed on the checkout page which gateway is being used. You may refresh the page to retry on the primary gateway.

Supported payment methods

  • UPI — PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, Cred, any UPI-enabled app
  • Debit and credit cards — Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, RuPay, AmEx (subject to issuing-bank approval)
  • Net Banking from 50+ Indian banks
  • Wallets — Paytm Wallet, PhonePe Wallet, Mobikwik, Amazon Pay
  • EMI on supported credit cards (for amounts above ₹2,000)
  • International cards for users transacting from outside India — subject to gateway support and applicable currency-conversion margins charged by your bank

Currency, GST and invoicing

  • All prices in Indian Rupees (₹ / INR), inclusive of GST at the applicable rate.
  • For user-supplied Indian GSTIN, a CGST-Rules-2017-compliant invoice (Form GST INV-1) is generated and emailed within 5 minutes of successful payment.
  • For users without a GSTIN, a Bill of Supply or B2C tax invoice is generated.
  • Invoices accessible from your account dashboard for 8 years from payment, per CGST Act §36.
  • Where you are established outside India and the supply qualifies as export of services under IGST Act §2(6), GST is charged at 0% subject to §16 conditions.

Transaction security

  • All payment transactions over TLS 1.2 or higher, with end-to-end encryption between your browser, the gateway and your card-issuing bank.
  • Card data (PAN, CVV, expiry) is never transmitted to or stored by us. The gateway tokenises the card and provides only a transaction reference.
  • Three-Domain-Secure (3DS) authentication is mandatorily invoked for all Indian-card transactions per RBI directions.
  • For UPI transactions, two-factor authentication via your UPI PIN is mandatory.

Failed transactions

  • A transaction may fail for many reasons — insufficient funds, expired card, mismatched details, bank-side outage, exceeded daily limit, gateway timeout.
  • Where it fails before debit, no further action required.
  • Where it fails after debit, the amount is auto-reversed by the gateway within 3–7 working days. No action required from you.
  • If reversal has not occurred within 7 working days, write to support@naamdekho.in.

Fallback gateway switching — Razorpay → Paytm

  • We continuously monitor Razorpay's health and response-time. Upon detection of degraded availability (error rate > 5% over a 5-minute window, or median response > 10 seconds), new checkout sessions automatically switch to Paytm.
  • You are informed of the gateway at checkout. Pending transactions on the primary gateway are not migrated — they complete or auto-reverse normally.
  • After 30 minutes of stable primary-gateway operation, new sessions revert to the primary.
  • In the rare event both gateways are simultaneously unavailable, the Platform displays a notice and does not levy any charge.

Subscription billing — Founder Pro & Agency

  • Recurring subscriptions billed in advance monthly (or as set out in the MSA for Agency Tier).
  • Auto-renewal is enabled by default at sign-up. You may disable it any time from the dashboard.
  • Failed renewals are retried up to 3 times over 5 days. If all retries fail, the subscription is suspended and you are notified by email.
  • Our recurring-billing mandate is established with the gateway in accordance with RBI directions on e-mandate processing dated 16 August 2019, as amended.

Cancellation, refunds and chargebacks
