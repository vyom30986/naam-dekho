01Pronunciation

Will the family say it the same way? Will their school friends? Will their first manager forty years from now? A name is the sound of itself, repeated tens of thousands of times.

Syllable break · stress on highlighted
vyaa naa
IPA /ˈvjɑː.nɑː/ · 2 syllables · open ending
✓
Easy on Indian tongues. The "vyaa-" cluster is natural in Sanskrit-rooted names. Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati and Telugu speakers will produce it correctly on first hearing.
!
Medium for Western tongues. English speakers may simplify the "vy-" cluster to "vee-". Acceptable, but be ready to gently correct.
♪
Open ending (-aa). Names ending in a long "aa" sound carry warmth and travel well across South Asian languages.

06Linguistic & cultural

India is multilingual. A name that's neutral in English can be unfortunate in Tamil or Bengali. This is the layer every Western naming tool skips.

Devanagari transliteration
व्याना
Sanskrit root: व्यान (vyāna) — one of the five vital airs (prāṇas) in Ayurveda governing circulation and full-body movement. Connotes flow, distribution, pervasion.

Pronunciation: vyaa-naa · 2 syllables · easy on Indian tongues, medium on Western (the "vy-" cluster trips some speakers).
